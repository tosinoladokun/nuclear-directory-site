About our crawler

NuclearDirectoryBot collects public information about the nuclear industry from official and company sources. It identifies itself as:

NuclearDirectoryBot/0.1 (+https://nuclear-directory.org)

  • It follows robots.txt.
  • It visits each source at most once a day and skips pages that haven’t changed.
  • If a site blocks it, it stops. It doesn’t try to get around blocks.
  • It only collects public information, never personal data.

Questions about the crawler, or want it to stay away from your site? Email hello@nuclear-directory.org.
